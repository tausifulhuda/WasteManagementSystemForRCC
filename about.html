SECONDARY TRANSFER STATIONS

Rajshahi City Corporation (RCC) has established multiple modern Secondary Transfer Stations (STSs) to manage daily municipal waste (~420 tons) hygienically. These stations, such as in Terokhadia, Kazla, Sapura, and Paba, act as intermediate storage points to efficiently transfer waste to the final landfill, aiming to reduce environmental pollution in residential areas.

Key details regarding Rajshahi's Secondary Transfer Stations include:
  • Modernization Initiative: RCC plans to construct 30 STSs in phases to upgrade waste management from traditional dumping.

  • Key Locations: Existing modern STSs are located in areas including Terokhadia, Kazla, Sapura, Paba, Seroil Railway Station, Kashiadanga, New Govt. Degree College (Padma), and Shalbagan.

  • Functionality: These stations serve as crucial nodes where waste from households and businesses is collected, stored temporarily, and then transferred, minimizing waste disposal in public areas.

  • Recent Developments: Recent initiatives include opening new stations to meet growing city demands, though operational challenges like fuel shortages have occasionally impacted waste removal.

These STSs are designed to improve the environment of Rajshahi city, which is known for its cleanliness, by ensuring scientific and efficient waste disposal.
